Location

Voluntary Donor Registration

Would you like to register as a voluntary blood donor?

This is optional. You can create your TAVYA account without becoming a donor now. You can activate your donor profile later.

Blood-centre testing determines the medically verified blood group.
🔐

Donor Contact Consent

Registering as a donor does not automatically share your contact information with people requesting blood. If a compatible blood or blood-component request is identified, TAVYA may notify consenting donors. Your contact information will only be disclosed according to your explicit consent and TAVYA's applicable privacy rules.

🔒

Your Information & Privacy

Your personal information is stored in protected TAVYA backend systems and is not publicly displayed. Donor contact information is not made publicly searchable. TAVYA uses your information only for platform functions such as account management, blood-request coordination, notifications, records and related services according to your consent and applicable privacy rules.
