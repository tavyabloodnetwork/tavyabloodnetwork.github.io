How TAVYA Works

TAVYA is designed to coordinate people, requests and blood-centre interactions while keeping consent and privacy central.

3

About TAVYA

Transfusion Access & Voluntary Assistance

Our Purpose

TAVYA Blood Network is being developed as a technology platform to facilitate voluntary blood donation coordination. It aims to help connect consenting donors with people seeking blood and blood components while providing a structured system for requests, matching, donation records, verification and appreciation.


Consent & Privacy

Donor participation is voluntary. Donors choose whether they are willing to be contacted and whether to respond to individual requests. Personal contact information should only be exchanged according to the platform's consent and privacy rules.


Medical Responsibility

TAVYA is a coordination platform. Blood-centre and qualified medical professionals determine donor eligibility, blood screening, compatibility and other medical requirements.
